## v1.0.0 — 2026-07-14

- First public release of `media-publish-check`.
- Supports Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Weixin Video Accounts and Kuaishou.
- Adds multi-modal evidence checks for video, audio, images, graphic posts, articles and links.
- Adds short-video six-way publication-label gate: fictional reenactment, AI-generated, marketing, repost, personal opinion or no label required.
- Adds source, copyright, AI, commercial, livestream-commerce, privacy and high-risk-topic checks.
- Adds strict cross-platform naming mode, platform-specific revisions and concise two-outcome review format.
- Adds text candidate scanner, regression tests and official recheck routes.
