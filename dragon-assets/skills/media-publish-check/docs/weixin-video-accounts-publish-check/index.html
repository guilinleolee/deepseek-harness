视频号发布前，先核对内容、素材和发布页。

一句话怎么用

使用 $media-publish-check 审核我上传的视频号视频。
请检查口播、字幕、封面、画面、音乐、AI 标注、转载授权、事实来源和发布页信息。
先给能否直接发、最多 3 个问题和可直接替换的修改稿。

发布前重点

画面与口播

检查首帧、封面文字、口播、字幕、图片、音乐、外部账号、二维码和可识别个人信息。

来源与权利

检查转载片段、配乐、字体、肖像、聊天截图、新闻信息和拍摄时间地点的依据。

AI 与演绎

核对 AI 图片、视频、配音、数字人、虚构演绎的使用范围，并提示需要如实说明的位置。

发布场景

检查标题、简介、封面和互动话术，避免把其他平台版本直接搬进视频号。

结果边界：Skill 给出已检查范围内的发布前建议。平台最终审核、音乐可用范围和发布页选项以发布当日页面与官方规则为准。
WEIXIN VIDEO ACCOUNTS REVIEW

Review your content, assets, and disclosures before posting to Weixin Video Accounts.

Use this for short videos, scripts, livestream clips, covers, captions, and graphic assets. The review states what was inspected and what evidence still needs confirmation.

Install the SkillHome

Use it in one prompt

Use $media-publish-check to review my Weixin Video Accounts video.
Check the script, captions, cover, visuals, music, AI labels, repost rights, factual sources, and publishing-page information.
Give me a decision, up to 3 issues, and replacement wording.

What to review

Visuals and spoken claims

Review opening frames, cover text, speech, captions, images, music, external accounts, QR codes, and identifiable people.

Sources and rights

Review clips, music, fonts, likenesses, screenshots, news claims, and date or location evidence.

AI and reenactments

Clarify AI image, video, voice, avatar, and reenactment use and where truthful disclosure is needed.

Publishing context

Adapt titles, descriptions, covers, and calls to action for this destination platform.

Scope: This is pre-publish guidance for inspected material. Final moderation, music availability, and publishing-page choices remain subject to current platform rules.
