会检查什么

内容与封面一致性

封面是否把 AI 图、示意画面或修图效果伪装成实拍；标题是否比证据说得更满。

真实分享与合作披露

样品、赠品、品牌邀请、赞助、返佣、带货关系和虚假种草风险。

图片、视频与素材权利

转载、翻译、截图、音乐、字体、出镜人、聊天记录和可识别个人信息。

站外信息

二维码、联系方式、外部账号、其他平台名称，以及带有搜索、添加、购买等动作的表达。

不能替代平台审核：没有完整画面、授权或合作材料时，结果只能是初审或要求补证，不能承诺发布结果。

Use $media-publish-check to review my Xiaohongshu post.
Check the title, body, cover, images/video, tags, AI labels, commercial relationship,
rights, and off-platform information. Give me a decision, exact locations, and revisions.

What it checks

Content and cover alignment

Whether a cover presents an AI image, illustration, or retouching as an authentic photograph, and whether a title overstates the evidence.

Authenticity and disclosure

Samples, gifts, brand invitations, sponsorships, affiliate links, product promotion, and deceptive seeding risk.

Visual and asset rights

Reposts, translations, screenshots, music, fonts, likenesses, chat records, and identifiable personal information.

Off-platform information

QR codes, contact details, external accounts, platform names, and search/add/buy calls-to-action.

Not a platform decision: Missing visuals, rights, or commercial evidence limit the result to an initial review.
