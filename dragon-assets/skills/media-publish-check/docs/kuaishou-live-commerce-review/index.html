一句话怎么用

使用 $media-publish-check 审核我的快手直播话术和商品卡。
请检查商品名称、价格、库存、赠品、抽奖、功效主张、营销标注、素材授权和站外交易。
只给能否直接用、最多 3 个问题和可直接替换的说法。

直播前重点

商品信息一致性

核对商品名称、规格、适用范围、价格、库存、赠品、活动门槛和商品卡是否一致。

承诺与证据

核对功效、收益、最低价、稀缺、时效和售后承诺是否有明确依据，避免无法证明的绝对化表达。

营销与关系披露

检查品牌合作、返佣、赠品、抽奖、互动条件和营销信息是否需要清楚说明。

素材与交易路径

检查音乐、图片、出镜人、商品素材、外部账号、私下交易和联系方式。

使用建议：把直播话术、商品卡截图、活动规则和已知授权信息一起提供，才能得到更完整的审核范围说明。
KUAISHOU LIVE & COMMERCE REVIEW

Review your product information and live selling script before you go live.

Use this for Kuaishou livestreams, product scripts, product cards, group deals, flash sales, gifts, giveaways, and livestream clips. It checks whether claims, assets, disclosures, and commercial details are supportable.

Install the SkillHome

Use it in one prompt

Use $media-publish-check to review my Kuaishou live selling script and product card.
Check product name, price, stock, gifts, giveaways, efficacy claims, marketing labels, asset rights, and external transactions.
Return a decision, up to 3 issues, and direct replacement wording.

What to review

Product consistency

Check names, specifications, applicable scope, price, stock, gifts, eligibility terms, and product-card consistency.

Claims and evidence

Check whether efficacy, earnings, lowest-price, scarcity, timing, and after-sales claims have clear support.

Marketing context

Review brand partnerships, commissions, gifts, giveaways, interaction conditions, and marketing disclosures.

Assets and transactions

Review music, images, presenters, product media, external accounts, private transactions, and contact details.

Useful input: Include the script, product-card images, promotion rules, and known rights information for a complete scope statement.
