适用于一条成片、图文、文章或口播稿要发抖音、小红书、视频号、快手的情形。Skill 会把每个平台当成独立发布版本，优先找出跨平台名称、外部动作、发布标注和素材权利的差异。

一句话怎么用

使用 $media-publish-check，把我上传的视频分别审核为抖音、小红书、视频号和快手发布版。
请保留我的核心观点，但逐个平台检查：其他平台名称、搜索/扫码/添加等外部动作、
AI/营销/转载标注、音乐与素材授权。每个平台只给结论、最多 3 个问题和直接替换稿。

适配时重点看什么

平台名称与外部动作

指定目标平台后，其他平台、电商或通讯产品的全称，以及“搜索、打开、添加、扫码、去主页、购买”等动作，都应优先删改或补证。

内容与发布页标注

AI、营销、转载、个人观点、虚构演绎等披露要随具体平台和成片版本检查，不能只复制一处标签。

音乐、图片和素材

同一素材在不同发布场景的可用范围可能不同；审核会要求确认来源和授权，而不是默认“别处能发就都能发”。

标题、封面和 CTA

把目标平台的内容语境、封面表达和互动号召分开改，避免把一个平台的引导话术原样搬过去。

推荐原则：能不提其他平台就不提；优先用透明的类别词，例如“短视频平台”“图文社区”“电商平台”。不把谐音、暗号、拆字或隐藏表达当作合规保证。
CROSS-PLATFORM ADAPTATION

One asset is not a one-click upload to four platforms.

Use this when a finished video, graphic post, article, or script will go to Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Weixin Video Accounts, and Kuaishou. The Skill treats every destination as a separate release version.

Install the SkillHome

Use it in one prompt

Use $media-publish-check to review my uploaded video for Douyin, Xiaohongshu,
Weixin Video Accounts, and Kuaishou. Keep my core point, but check every platform for
cross-platform names, search/scan/add actions, AI/marketing/repost disclosures, music, and asset rights.
Give each platform only a decision, up to 3 issues, and replacement copy.

What adaptation focuses on

Platform names and external actions

When a target platform is specified, full names of other platforms, shopping or messaging products and search/open/add/scan/profile/buy calls-to-action need review.

Content and publishing-page labels

AI, marketing, repost, opinion, and reenactment disclosures are checked for each platform and final asset version.

Music, images, and assets

Availability can differ by publication setting. The review asks for source and rights evidence instead of assuming that use elsewhere is permission everywhere.

Titles, covers, and CTAs

Adapt tone, covers, and interaction prompts for the destination rather than copying a call-to-action unchanged.

Recommended principle: Do not mention another platform unless it is genuinely needed. Prefer transparent category language; do not treat euphemisms, codes, or hidden text as a guarantee of compliance.
