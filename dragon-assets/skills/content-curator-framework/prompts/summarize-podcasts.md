# Remix Podcast Transcripts

## Goal

Summarize a podcast episode's transcript into a 200-400 word overview that gives listeners the core insight, key arguments, and practical takeaway. Write for a non-expert audience — they should understand the episode without listening to it.

## Rules

### The Takeaway (MUST lead with this)
Start your summary with "The Takeaway:" as the very first sentence. This is non-negotiable.

The takeaway sentence should be a single, sharp, quotable insight that captures what listeners should remember.

### Structure
1. **The Takeaway** (1 sentence) — sharp, quotable insight
2. **Context** (1-2 sentences) — who the guest is, why this topic matters right now
3. **Key Points** (3-5 sentences) — the main arguments, framed for practitioners
4. **Notable Quote** (1 quote, in context) — a memorable line from the transcript
5. **Practical Implication** (1-2 sentences) — what this means for builders/researchers

### Word Count
200-400 words total. Prefer 250-350 for balance.

### Content Rules
- Do NOT copy sentences verbatim from the transcript. Paraphrase completely.
- Include **specific numbers, named features, company names** where they appear.
- Write for a smart reader who doesn't need concepts explained from scratch, but appreciates practical implications.
- Skip tangents, lengthy biographical backstories, and sponsor reads.
- If the episode covers multiple topics, focus on the most actionable one.

### Must Include
- Podcast name, episode title, and episode URL from the JSON (NOT from the transcript)
- One memorable direct quote (in quotes, with attribution)
- A clear practical implication for practitioners in AI/ML

### Output Format

```
[Podcast Name] — [Episode Title](URL)
The Takeaway: [sharply quotable 1-sentence insight]

[200-400 word summary paragraph]

"The [memorable quote from the episode]." — [Guest Name]

Practical implication: [what builders/researchers should do with this insight]
```
