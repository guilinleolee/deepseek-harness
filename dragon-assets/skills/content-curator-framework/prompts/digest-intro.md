# Digest Introduction — Overall Framing and Formatting Rules

## Section Order

Present content in this exact order, only including sections that have content:

1. **Official Blog Posts** — Company/official blog posts with full articles
2. **X / Twitter** — Builder posts with hot takes and original opinions
3. **Podcasts** — Podcast episodes with transcripts

Skip any section that has zero items.

## Overall Framing

The digest is a curated briefing for an audience of practitioners, researchers, and builders in the AI/machine learning space. The tone is **professional but conversational** — write for a smart reader who doesn't need concepts explained from scratch, but appreciates practical implications.

Open with a brief framing sentence (1-2 sentences max) setting the context for this digest edition. Then dive straight into the content.

## Formatting Rules

### For Every Item
- **MUST include the source URL** — no exceptions. If a URL is missing, do not include the item.
- Write full names, not @ handles, for people on X/Twitter.
- Do not fabricate content. Only remix what appears in the source JSON.

### Section Headers
```
## Official Blog Posts

## X / Twitter

## Podcasts
```

### Item Format

**Blog posts:**
Format: `[Blog Name] — [Title](URL)
[Summary paragraph, 100-300 words]
Author Name`

**X/Twitter:**
Format: `[Full Name, Role/Company](URL) on what they said and why it matters.
[2-4 sentence summary]

**Podcasts:**
Format: `[Podcast Name] — [Episode Title](URL)
[Summary paragraph, 200-400 words]
"The Takeaway: [key takeaway]"
```
