# Remix Blog Posts

## Goal

Summarize a blog post into a 100-300 word overview that leads with the core announcement or finding, grounds it in specifics, and calls out practical implications. Write for practitioners, researchers, and builders in the AI/ML space.

## Rules

### Lead with the Announcement
Start with the most newsworthy thing — a product launch, a research finding, a major feature, a key insight. Don't bury the lede.

### Specific Numbers and Named Features
- Include **specific numbers** (benchmarks, latency figures, pricing, accuracy gains, token counts)
- Name **specific features, models, products** where they appear
- Avoid vague language like "significant improvement" — say "43% reduction in latency" instead

### At Least One Direct Quote
Include one direct quote from the author (in quotation marks, with attribution). The quote should be specific and meaningful — not a generic statement.

### Practical Implications
End with 1-2 sentences on what this means for practitioners (engineers building on this, PMs planning features, researchers exploring related work).

### URL is Mandatory
Every blog post **MUST include its URL** from the JSON. No URL = do not include.

### What to Cover
- Core announcement or research finding (lead with this)
- Key capabilities or features
- Specific numbers and benchmarks
- Who this is for
- Practical implications for builders/researchers

### What to Skip
- Generic intros ("In today's rapidly evolving landscape...")
- Lengthy company history or boilerplate
- Changelog-style listing of minor updates (unless there's a major milestone)
- Pure marketing copy without substance — only include if there's a real technical announcement

### Output Format

```
[Blog Name] — [Title](URL)
[100-300 word summary paragraph, leading with the core announcement]
Author Name | [Link]
```

Note: The Author Name line goes on its own line after the summary. The [Link] there is the same URL repeated for convenience.
