# Link Building Reference

## Link Quality Metrics Guide

### Domain Authority (Moz)
| DA Range | Quality | Effort to Earn |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| 70+ | Premium | Very High |
| 50-69 | High | High |
| 30-49 | Medium | Medium |
| 10-29 | Low | Low |
| <10 | Minimal | Easy but low value |

### Trust Flow vs Citation Flow
| Pattern | Meaning |
|---------|--------|
| TF > CF | Quality links |
| CF > TF | Potentially manipulative |
| TF = CF | Balanced profile |

## Link Building Methods

### 1. Guest Posting
**Best for**: Building authority, contextual links
**Success Rate**: 5-15%
**Effort**: Medium

### 2. Resource Pages
**Best for**: Niche-specific links
**Success Rate**: 10-30%
**Effort**: Low-Medium

### 3. Broken Link Building
**Best for**: Quick wins
**Success Rate**: 15-25%
**Effort**: Medium

### 4. Digital PR
**Best for**: High authority, brand mentions
**Success Rate**: 20-40%
**Effort**: High

### 5. HARO/Connectively
**Best for**: Expert positioning
**Success Rate**: 30-50%
**Effort**: Low

## Outreach Email Templates

### Guest Post Pitch
```
Subject: Guest Post Proposal - [Unique Angle]

Hi [Name],

I came across [site] and really enjoyed your recent article on [topic].

I'm [Your Name], a [Your Title] who's been covering [industry] for [X years]. I've contributed to [Publication 1], [Publication 2], and [Publication 3].

I'd love to contribute a guest post on: [Specific, Timely Topic]

Quick outline:
- [Point 1 - one sentence]
- [Point 2 - one sentence]
- [Point 3 - one sentence]

Would love to share this with your audience.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Title] | [Website]
[Social Profile]
```

### Broken Link Building
```
Subject: Quick Fix - Broken Link on [Page Name]

Hi [Name],

I was researching [topic] and found your article "[Page Title]" very helpful.

I noticed one of your links isn't working anymore:
[Broken URL] → 404 Error

I actually have a similar resource that might be a good replacement:
[Your Resource URL]

It's a [brief description]. Would you consider updating the link?

Thanks for your time!

[Your Name]
```
