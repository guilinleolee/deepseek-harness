# Keyword Research Templates

## Seed Keyword Expansion Checklist

### Question Modifiers
- How to / How do / How can
- What is / What are / What does
- Why do / Why is / Why does
- Where is / Where to / Where can
- When to / When should
- Who should / Who is
- Which is / Which one
- Is / Are / Can / Does

### Long-tail Patterns
- [product] for [use case]
- [product] vs [alternative]
- best [product] for [audience]
- [product] near me
- [product] [location]
- cheap/fordable [product]
- top [number] [product]
- [product] with [feature]

## Intent Classification Guide

| Intent | User Query Examples | Content Type |
|--------|-------------------|--------------|
| Informational | "how to", "what is", "why does" | Blog, Guide, Tutorial |
| Navigational | brand names, product names | Homepage, Product Page |
| Transactional | "buy", "price", "discount", "coupon" | Product Page, CTA |
| Commercial | "best", "top", "review", "compare" | Comparison, Review |
